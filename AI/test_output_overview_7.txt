=== GreenHome Energy Saver ===
Summary:
GreenHome is a smart home system that tracks energy usage and provides recommendations to reduce consumption. It integrates with appliances and offers real-time alerts for inefficiencies. The platform aims to lower utility bills and promote sustainability.

Alerts

Enable AI recommendations
