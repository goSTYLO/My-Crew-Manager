Epic 1: Create comprehensive telemedicine platform
-Sub-Epic 1: Planning Phase
-User Story 1: As a Developer, I want to design secure video visits for patients so they can receive medical care remotely without leaving their homes. This will improve patient outcomes by reducing travel time and increasing access to specialists who are hard to reach otherwise.
-Task 1: Research security protocols and best practices for remote consultations.
-Task 2: Develop a prototype of a secure video consultation system using established technologies such as webcams, encryption algorithms, and authentication methods.
Epic 2: Improve patient outcomes
-Sub-Epic 1: Implementation Phase
-User Story 1: As a Healthcare Professional, I want to be able to schedule appointments through our new telemedicine platform so we can manage our workload more efficiently. Additionally, I would like to have access to shared patient records via the platform. This will help me provide better care to my patients while saving time spent searching for information online.
-Task 1: Set up appointment scheduling functionality within the telemedicine platform.
-Task 2: Implement sharing of patient records between providers.
Epic 3: Reduce healthcare costs
-Sub-Epic 1: Deployment and Monitoring Phase
-User Story 1: As a Patient, I want to use the telemedicine platform to communicate with my doctor about my health concerns. The AI should assist me in identifying potential issues based on my symptoms. Furthermore, I need easy access to my own personal health data stored securely on the platform. This will allow me to make informed decisions regarding my treatment plan and follow-up appointments.
-Task 1: Ensure compatibility with various devices and platforms for seamless usage.
-Task 2: Implement an AI-powered symptom checker integrated into the telemedicine platform.
-End Task: Deploy the complete telemedicine platform including all features and functionalities outlined above.
